# Two different things are called "crawling", and they deserve opposite answers.
#
# Search — including AI search — sends people back. Being read by Google, Bing,
# ChatGPT search or Perplexity is how a small business gets found at all, so
# those are welcome without conditions. That is the whole point of AEO.
#
# Harvesting takes and returns nothing: model-training crawlers and site
# copiers. Those are refused. A refusal in robots.txt is only honoured by
# crawlers that choose to honour it — the ones that ignore it are handled at the
# edge, not here — but the well-behaved majority does read this file, and the
# ones named below publicly commit to obeying it.
